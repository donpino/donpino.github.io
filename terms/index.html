The terms of an audit and of the fixes

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The service is simple, so these terms are short. Asking me for an audit, or for the fixes, means agreeing to them. If anything here is unclear, email me before you ask.

Who you are dealing with

DropoffAudit is run by Luca Panetto, an individual based in Italy. Postal address and contact details are in the privacy notice. Email: luca@dropoffaudit.me.

The audit, free

  • One written audit of the public website you ask me to look at: the two to four things standing between a visitor and a booking or a sale, the exact words to change, and the order to do them in.
  • It costs nothing, and it is yours to keep whether or not you ask me for anything else.
  • It reaches you by email, as a PDF, within three business days of my reply to your request. I do a limited number of audits each week. If I cannot take yours, or there is a wait, I say so in that reply.
  • The audit marks which leaks can be changed from your site’s editor and which cannot, such as your Google reviews, what Google’s AI says about your business, or a limit in a booking tool run by another company.

The fixes

  • If you ask for them, I make the changes myself for every leak in the audit that can be changed from your site’s editor. For the other leaks you get written steps. They are not part of the promise below.
  • Access. You invite me to your site as a collaborator, from your own account, in the way your site builder provides. I do not ask for your password or for customer data. You can remove my access at any time, and I ask you to remove it when the work is done. If your site cannot give that kind of access, I tell you before starting, and we do not start.
  • Seven days. The changes are made within seven days of the day the access works. If I need an answer from you to go on, the days I wait for it do not count.
  • What I change. The changes written in the audit. If something else is needed, I ask you first. I keep a record of every change, with what was there before and what is there now, and I send it to you, so that anything can be put back.
  • The recheck. When the changes are made I measure every leak again, the same way as in the audit, and send you what it was and what it is now.

Price and payment

The fixes cost $990, paid once, through Stripe. You pay after the recheck, and only if every leak covered by the promise is closed. If one of them is still open, you pay nothing. The changes I made stay on your site.

The first five businesses pay nothing. In exchange I ask for an honest review, which I may publish with your agreement, and for your booking or order numbers before and after.

There is no subscription and nothing renews. I never see or store your card details. Since you pay only after the result has been measured, there is no refund to ask for. If you pay as a consumer, any right the law gives you applies in addition to these terms.

What this is, and what it is not

  • The audit describes what I saw on your public website, on the dates written in it. Websites change, and so can what the audit describes.
  • Neither the audit nor the fixes promise any number of bookings, customers or revenue, or any position on Google.
  • A leak is “closed” when the measurement written in the audit, repeated on your live site, no longer shows it. That is what the recheck reports.
  • For the audit I look only at what any visitor can see. For the fixes I use only the access you give me, and only to make the changes described above.
  • You decide what gets changed. If you would rather not have one of the changes made, tell me before I start. That leak is then left out of the promise.
  • None of this is legal, medical or regulatory advice, including on the rules for advertising medical treatments.

Using the audit

The audit is yours to use in your business and to share with your staff, your web designer or anyone who helps you act on it. I do not publish your audit, and I do not name your business anywhere, unless you agree to it in writing.

Responsibility

If something goes wrong on my side, my responsibility is limited to the price of the fixes, $990, except where the law does not allow that limit.

Applicable law

These terms are governed by Italian law.

Changes

If I change these terms I will update the date at the top. Work you have already asked for stays under the terms that were in force when you asked.
