Who writes this site, and how

Last updated: 13 November 2026

DropoffAudit is one person. I’m Luca Panetto, and I run it on my own from northern Italy. I audit business websites to find where visitors give up, and I am responsible for everything published here.

What I do

A website conversion audit: I go through a site the way a new customer would, and write down where the way to a booking or a purchase stops working. Every finding is something seen on the live page, with the page and the date. You can read a full sample audit and what an audit is.

The service is new. I have no client results to show, so there are none on this site.

How the articles are written

  • Each one starts from a claim people repeat and goes back to where it was first measured.
  • A source is cited only after it was opened and read. When only part of a source could be read, the article says which part.
  • Every figure comes with how it was measured and on how many people: an experiment, a survey, a company report.
  • When a source sells a related product, the article says so.
  • Each article says what its sources do not show, in a section of its own.

How I use AI

I work with an AI assistant, and I would rather say so. It helps to search for studies, to read them and to draft the text. It works under the same rule as the rest of the site: nothing is cited that was not opened and read, and nothing is stated that cannot be traced to a source or to something seen on a live page.

What you will not find here

  • Advertising, affiliate links or sponsored articles.
  • Statistics without a source, or an industry average presented as your number.
  • A promise that an audit will raise your sales.

If something is wrong

If you find an error in an article or in the sample audit, email luca@dropoffaudit.me. I check it against the source and correct the page.

Contact and legal details

Email: luca@dropoffaudit.me. I answer myself. My postal address is in the privacy notice, and what you buy when you buy an audit is in the terms.
