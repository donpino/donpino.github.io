A real audit, in full

The document a client receives

This is a complete audit of a real medical spa website in Idaho, written the way every paid audit is written. The clinic’s name is withheld and no street address appears, but everything else is exactly what they would get: four leaks, three things already working, and the order I would fix them in.

  • Seven pages, PDF. Cover, strengths, one page per leak, and a closing order of work.
  • Checked on the live site on 12 and 18 September 2026. Each leak says when and how it was measured. A finding that could not be confirmed on the second visit was left out.
  • What it found. A booking page still listing the template’s sample services, three booking buttons that lead to a message form instead of a calendar, a homepage that shows no patient review, and a screen of empty space above the footer.
  • What it does not contain. Any percentage, any revenue estimate, any borrowed industry figure. The impact of each leak is described, not invented.
