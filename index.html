How it’s checked

Reading the code isn’t the same as looking

Text can sit in a page’s code and be invisible on the screen: grey letters on a dark photograph, or a button parked off the edge of the canvas where nobody will ever reach it.

Twice while preparing recent audits I found a promising problem this way and then deleted it, because measuring the element proved a real visitor never sees it. This is the slow part, and it is why the audits are short.

  • Every finding is confirmed against the page as it renders, not against the source code.
  • If it can’t be verified, it doesn’t go in, even when it would make the audit look better.
  • No invented statistics, and no industry average dressed up as your number.

The audit arrives by email, once, as a PDF. There is no dashboard to log into and nothing renews.

Not a med spa? I audit other practices that take bookings, such as dental, aesthetic, cosmetic surgery and wellness clinics. Email me and I’ll tell you whether it’s a fit.

What I can’t tell you

The whole promise here is that I don’t make things up. It would be strange to start on this page.

  • I can’t promise the audit will increase your bookings. I can promise the problems in it are real, and that I found them on your site.

  • I use AI, and I’d rather say so. It helps on some of the work. Nothing in the audit rests on it alone.
  • I won’t phone you. No discovery call, no follow-up sequence. You reply to the email and I answer it myself.
